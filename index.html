🎄 Family Secret Santa

Collect wishlists, add everyone, mark who's married to whom, and draw names. Each person gets their own private link that only shows their match — so even the organizer stays surprised.

1. Collect wishlists

Send this link to everyone in the group chat. Each person fills in gift ideas and things they like, then texts the link it gives them back to you. When you open their link on this device, they're added to the list below with their wishlist.

Organizing from another phone or computer too? and open it there.

2. Who's playing?

People who sent a wishlist show up here. You can also add names yourself and type in wishlists for them.

    Spouses won't be matched with each other. Use the dropdown next to each name above to pick their partner.
